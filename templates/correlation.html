Understanding Correlation

Correlation value: The correlation coefficient indicates the strength and direction of the relationship between two numerical variables.

A value closer to +1 indicates a stronger positive relationship, while a value closer to -1 indicates a stronger negative relationship.

Values closer to 0 indicate a weaker linear relationship.

In this project, the analysis examines how attendance, study hours, subject marks and internal average are related to final marks.
